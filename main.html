Health Insights

Long-term obesity trends in Australia reveal increasing health concerns among both males and females. Changes in dietary habits, fast food accessibility, and lifestyle behaviours may contribute to these patterns over time.

Age-group comparisons also show higher obesity and overweight rates among middle-aged adults, highlighting the growing impact of unhealthy eating habits across different stages of life.
